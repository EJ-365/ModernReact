Why it looks real

Not a handwriting font. A hand.

A font repeats the exact same “a” every time — that’s what gives fake handwriting away. Penwright simulates the writer, the pen, the paper and the camera.

No two letters alike

Every glyph gets its own rotation, size, slant and subtle warp, so repeated letters never match.

Lines that drift

Baselines wobble, whole lines tilt, and the left margin wanders the way it does when you write freehand.

Real ink behaviour

Ballpoint skips and blobs, gel saturates, fountain ink pools at stroke ends, pencil catches the paper tooth.

Pressure & fatigue

Ink darkness varies stroke to stroke, and writing gets a little bigger and looser further down the page.

Human mistakes

Optional misspellings scribbled out and rewritten, plus faint smudges where a palm brushed the ink.

Paper you can feel

College, wide and A4 ruled, legal pads, graph, dot grid, index cards, parchment — with grain, holes and torn edges.

Scanned or photographed

Add flatbed scan skew, photocopy toner, or a phone photo on an oak desk with perspective, light and shadow.

How it works

Three steps. About thirty seconds.

  1. 1

    Bring your text

    Type, paste, or drop in a Word, PDF, text, Markdown, OpenDocument or RTF file. Headings, bullets and paragraphs carry over.

  2. 2

    Choose the hand

    Pick a handwriting, pen, ink colour and paper. Add your name and date to the header like a real assignment.

  3. 3

    Download

    Get a multi-page PDF, or PNG/JPG images (zipped for multiple pages) at up to 300 dpi — or print it directly.

Honest limits

What Penwright can’t do

  • Read scanned pages. Image-only PDFs and photos have no text inside. OCR them first.
  • Open old .doc files. Re-save them as .docx in Word or Google Docs.
  • Copy your exact hand from a photo. Turn your writing into a font with a free tool like Calligraphr, then upload it.
  • Install from Firefox. Firefox on desktop can’t install web apps. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari, or keep using it in a tab.
  • Write every script. The bundled hands cover Latin letters and accents. Arabic, CJK, Cyrillic and others need a custom font that includes them.
  • Keep tables, images or exact layout. Imports bring in the words, headings and lists. Everything is rewritten onto ruled paper.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Penwright really free?

Yes. There are no accounts, watermarks or page limits. Everything runs in your browser, so there’s nothing to pay for on our side.

Can I install it on my computer or phone?

Yes. Click Install app at the top of the page. In Chrome or Edge (Windows, Mac, Linux, ChromeOS, Android) Penwright installs in one click. On iPhone and iPad use Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Safari on a Mac use File, then Add to Dock. It opens in its own window with its own icon, and keeps working with no internet. You can also open Word, PDF and text files with it straight from your file manager.

Can I use my own handwriting?

Yes. Create a font from a photo of your handwriting with a free tool like Calligraphr, then upload the .ttf/.otf in the Hand tab. Penwright applies all the same natural variation to it, and remembers it on this device.

Will it print at the right size?

PDFs are generated at the exact paper size (US Letter, A4, A5, Legal, index card or sticky note). Choose “Digital” finish and print at 100% / actual size for the most natural result on real paper.

Why did my PDF import come out empty?

Scanned PDFs are pictures of text, so there’s no text inside to extract. Run them through OCR first (e.g. Google Drive → Open with Google Docs), or export the original document as .docx or .pdf.

Which languages and characters work?

Latin-script languages including accents (é, ñ, ü, ø…) are supported. Characters a handwriting doesn’t include are swapped for close equivalents, like curly quotes to straight ones.

Can I submit this as handwritten homework?

Please don’t. If an assignment must be handwritten by you, submitting generated pages may break your school’s academic-integrity rules. Penwright is great for study notes, mockups, cards, worksheets and design work.
