Creator

I built Houston Traffic Simulator as a living postcard of Space City — and it grew into a seven-metro atlas. Austin, San Antonio, Dallas–Fort Worth, Los Angeles, New York, and Boston now share the same engine, each with its own freeways, weather mosaic, and sky.

Why this exists

Houston isn’t a postcard grid. It’s spaghetti freeways, sudden storms, and a sky that never sleeps. I wanted a simulator that feels like here, not a generic city skin.

Every district label, flood corridor, and runway rabbit is a small thank you to the metro that raised the idea, and a template for the cities that follow.

What I care about

  • Live data when it makes the city truer
  • Honest modeling when APIs can’t reach
  • UI that stays cute without getting in the way
  • A multi city platform that still feels local

Seven metros live

Live now: Houston, Austin, San Antonio, Dallas–Fort Worth, Los Angeles, New York, and Boston. Next up: Chicago, Miami, Seattle, and more as the platform grows.

Same craft for every metro: suburb-local weather, traffic context, airports, landmarks, and hazard overlays tuned to that place — not a copy-paste skyline.
