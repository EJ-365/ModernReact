FAQ

Quick answers

Is it free?

Yes. Public and free-to-access sources — no paid traffic or weather subscription required.

Are all speeds live?

Freeway speeds come from Houston TranStar where sensors exist. Elsewhere traffic is simulated from time of day, weather and road type, and the app labels it as modeled.

How do flights work?

Positions come from free ADS-B. Cards link out to airplanes.live for deeper tracking — one flight number, not a duplicate ICAO twin.

What is modeled?

Roads, buildings, airports and landmarks come from OpenStreetMap. Some details, like suburban houses and street life, are generated to fill the map. Weather, alerts and aircraft update live.
