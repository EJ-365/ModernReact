FAQ

Quick answers

Is it free?

Yes. It uses only free and open data and services. There is no paid traffic, weather or map subscription, and no account.

Are all speeds live?

Freeway speeds come from Houston TranStar where sensors exist. Elsewhere traffic is simulated from time of day, weather and road type, and the app labels it as modeled.

How do flights work?

Positions come from free ADS-B feeds. Flight cards link out to airplanes.live for deeper tracking.

What is modeled?

Roads, buildings, airports and landmarks come from OpenStreetMap. Some details, like suburban houses, are generated to fill the map. Weather, alerts and aircraft update live.
