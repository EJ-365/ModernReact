FAQ

Quick answers

Is it free?

Yes. Public and free-to-access sources — no paid traffic or weather subscription required.

Are all speeds live?

Houston uses TranStar where available. Other metros use clearly labeled modeled traffic so the map stays active without pretending estimates are measured.

How do flights work?

Positions come from free ADS-B. Cards link out to airplanes.live for deeper tracking — one flight number, not a duplicate ICAO twin.

What is modeled?

Road geometry, districts, airports, and landmarks are researched per city. Weather, alerts, news, and aircraft update from their feeds.
